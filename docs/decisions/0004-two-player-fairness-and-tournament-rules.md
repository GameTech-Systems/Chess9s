# 0004: 2-player fairness and tournament rules

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision
Fair play is required for 2-player games, and it is secured **when the default rules are made**: the default 2-player ruleset is designed and checked for fairness before it is released, and the evidence is published with it. **Official tournament rules** are then established after a public discussion period, followed by ratification by the project owner.

## Consequences
- The default ruleset is published with status `candidate` once it is fairness-checked, and is open to public discussion.
- If discussion changes a ruleset from the fairness-checked default, the changed ruleset is re-checked for fairness before ratification.
- Other 2-player variants may be offered as `experimental` options for casual play. They are not tournament rules.
- 3-player rules stay experimental until testing shows they are balanced.
