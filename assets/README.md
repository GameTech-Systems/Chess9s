## Released art

`brand/` was released by the Owner on 3 November 2026 for chess9s.com (decision 0024). The web app links to these files; it does not license them.

- `brand/emblem.svg`: the phoenix emblem, a glowing egg with nine feathers on each wing. The logo, and the symbol on the Ting Yang square (i5).
- `brand/favicon.svg`, `brand/favicon-32.png`, `brand/apple-touch-icon.png`: the emblem as the site icon.
- `brand/pieces/`: the five Coast pieces (Light Dragon Head, Sun, Dragon Tail, Moon, Dark Dragon Head) as turned board-game pieces. The three Dragon pieces stand on hollow tetrahedral bases that fit over the Center (e5).

The game board itself still draws the pieces as plain lettered discs.
